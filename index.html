    Eulogy

    Birth

    The late Rael Kasuvile Ndeve was born in 1923 in Ngaku Village, Mutyambua Sub-Location, Mutyambua Location, Mbitini Division, Nzaui Sub-County, Makueni County.

    She was the second-born daughter of the late Kivonde Kilaki and the late Kakene Kivonde. She was a sister to the late Mbaika Longe and a stepsister to Nzambuli, Mukonzi, Mbindyo, the late Kamene, and the late Mbula. She was also an aunt to Benard Longe and Ruth Kimondiu.

    Education

    Mwaitu had no formal education.

    Christian Life

    The late Rael was a staunch Christian. She attended A.I.C. Ikalaasa, where she was baptized, and later joined A.B.C. Masue.

    Marriage

    The late Rael was married to the late Daud Ndeve Ndone through a traditional marriage in 1943. They were blessed with seven children, namely: Jackson Mwania, Alice Katulu Kisuvai, the late Kivuva, the late Muthoki, Joseph Kioko, Eliud Matheka, and Grace Mumbi (Musengy'a) Sila.

    She was a mother-in-law to the late Phyllis Katule Mwania, Christine Mwania, Elizabeth Kioko, and Dorothy Matheka.

    Sisters-in-Law

    Her sisters-in-law were Ruth Nzamalu, Elizabeth Nguluutu Nzungili, the late Elizabeth Ngova, Mary Nduleve Masuli, the late Susan Kaumbu, and the late Elizabeth Nzilani Mutavuta.

    Sons-in-Law

    She had two sons-in-law, namely the late Kisuvai Silu and Benjamin Sila.

    Grandchildren

    The late Rael was blessed with 36 grandchildren, namely: Tom, Munyao, Kimanthi, Mwikali, Kyalo, Kasiku, Ndeve (Nau), Muthini, Kisilu, Mercy, Ndinda, Nduki, Mutua, Mutinda, Mutisya, Mwongela, Mwendwa, Mutanu, Mumo, Musengya, Muuo, Nzioki, Mutunga, Kennedy, the late Nduku, Katungwa, Peter, Wambua, Katiwa, Somba, Silu, Mumbua, Muoki, Kimeu, Mutola, Muteti, and Purity.

    Great-Grandchildren / Great-Great-Grandchildren

    She left behind 60 great-grandchildren and 13 great-great-grandchildren.

    Career

    The late Rael was a farmer and a devoted housewife who was fully committed to caring for her family and household. She was hardworking and dedicated to the well-being of those around her.

    Illness and Demise

    God blessed Rael Kasuvile with good health, and she lived a vibrant and active life from childhood. She remained hardworking and supportive of the many people who came across her path.

    In her later years, she experienced some minor health challenges, which were attended to at various health facilities, including Sultan Hamud Level 4 Hospital and Machakos Level 5 Hospital.

    She had been well in the days leading up to her passing. On 9th September 2026, she was assisted by her son to go to bed. In the process, she developed some minor complications and peacefully went to be with the Lord.

    God gives and God takes.
    Praise His Name. Amen!
